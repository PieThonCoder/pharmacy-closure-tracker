Openings and closures by year, from the board's own export

Unlike most boards, Massachusetts' bulk export keeps closed licenses in the file with a dated terminal status. As of the 2026-08-22 export: 1,020 Current retail pharmacy licenses; 916 Out of Business (every one carrying a closure date, the earliest 2008; the table below starts at 2013); 17 Surrendered, 8 Revoked, 4 Void; and 2,671 Expired (almost all with expiry dates before 2015; only 13 expired 2015 or later without an Out-of-Business flag).

* partial year. † administrative batch, not market churn: 2018: 78% of 2018 issuances are dated month 01, and those licenses carry a new number format (DS#####) where earlier ones are DS####. A month-concentrated block of issuances with a new number format is the signature of a licensing-system changeover (numbers re-issued, old ones closed administratively); do not read that year as openings or closures. In 2016 more than half of the year's closures are dated December, consistent with year-end store closures. "Licenses issued" counts every license in the export by its issue year, including ones that later closed; issued and closed are the board's own dates. "Issued minus recorded exits" is not a net change in operating pharmacies: exits that were never flagged Out of Business (plain expiries) are not counted, and batch years distort both sides. Whether a license marked Out of Business closed on exactly that date is the board's determination, not ours.

The two-year-bucket problem, and what the tracker adds

The Massachusetts Health Policy Commission's DataPoints #31 (October 2025), mapping pharmacy deserts from the state's license verification site, notes:

Two things follow. First, the board's bulk export (this page's source) already dates a large share of closures to the day via the Out-of-Business status - 916 of them - so a closure series finer than two-year buckets is available for that subset from the export itself. Second, closures that are never flagged Out of Business remain invisible until the biennial December-31 expiry, exactly as the HPC describes; the only way to place those inside the two-year window from public bulk data is to watch the roster continuously and record the week a license drops or its status changes. That is what this page does, weekly, from 2026-08-08 forward. The HPC excluded compounding, specialty and provider-based pharmacies not open to the public; this page counts every type-80 retail pharmacy license as the board classifies it, without further filtering.

About this board's data

  • Massachusetts' Board of Registration in Pharmacy exposes a no-login bulk export of retail pharmacy licenses (recorded weekly here) that retains non-current licenses with dated terminal statuses (Out of Business with a closure date; Surrendered / Revoked / Void with dates). Licenses renew biennially and expire on December 31 of odd years.
