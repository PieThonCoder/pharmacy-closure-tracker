About this board's data

  • West Virginia's Board of Pharmacy facility roster is a plain CSV with status and expiration date but no original issue date, so renewal re-listings cannot be distinguished from new licenses here (appearances may be over-counted around renewal season).

Primary source

  • West Virginia Board of Pharmacy - facility roster (weekly)

Method, caveats and the meaning of each event type: tracker method. Removal from a roster is not proof of closure.
