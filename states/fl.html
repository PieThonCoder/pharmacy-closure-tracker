About this board's data

  • Florida's Department of Health (MQA) business-search export lists in-state pharmacy permits with a status and street address and retains closed permits: at the first snapshot, 11,025 Clear (the active status) alongside 10,896 Closed, 4,351 Null And Void, 414 Vol Relinquish, 192 Revoked, 152 Disc Relinquish and a few others. Closures should therefore appear here as status changes rather than removals - not yet observed, since recording began 2026-08-16. No issue or closure dates are in the export.

Primary source

  • Florida DOH MQA - pharmacy permits export, all statuses (weekly; recorded from 2026-08-16)

Method, caveats and the meaning of each event type: tracker method. Removal from a roster is not proof of closure.
