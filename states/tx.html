About this board's data

  • The Texas State Board of Pharmacy rebuilds phydsk.csv daily and keeps no history; a license that closes is deleted from the file, usually while still marked Active. Recorded daily since 2026-08-08; 49 Internet Archive captures 2014-2025 were replayed for the twelve-year analysis.
  • Fields: license number, name, address, county, status, original license date, class, ownership type. Out-of-state (nonresident) pharmacies are in the same file and are flagged here by their address state.

Primary source

  • Texas State Board of Pharmacy - site licenses (daily)

Method, caveats and the meaning of each event type: tracker method. Removal from a roster is not proof of closure.
