Data dictionary

What is in the free files, how events are defined, and what the full archive behind them contains.

Free files (CC BY 4.0)

  • data/latest-week.csv - every event in the trailing 7-day window across all recorded jurisdictions (rebuilt daily).
  • data/since-2026-08-08.csv - cumulative event log since recording began.
  • data/weeks/<saturday>.csv - dated, permalinked copies of the weekly log (one per Saturday from 2026-08-15), e.g. 2026-09-05.
  • states/data/<st>-latest-week.csv and <st>-since-2026-08-08.csv - the same, per jurisdiction (linked from each state page).
  • data/latest.json - machine-readable summary: window, totals, per-state counts, the removed-while-Active rows with their NPPES cross-check result, refused sources.
  • feed.xml - RSS, one item per ISO week.

Cite as: Pharmacy Closure Tracker, https://piethoncoder.github.io/pharmacy-closure-tracker/, retrieved YYYY-MM-DD. Please link the page you used.

Event definitions (short form; full method)

  • New license (appear): license number present in the later snapshot and absent from the earlier one, with an issue date consistent with a new issuance (or no issue dates published by that board).
  • Removed from roster (disappear): present earlier, absent later. Not asserted as a closure. In the Texas replay only 7 of 5,871 removals (0.1%) were later seen on the roster again; of the 5,864 that never returned, 92.7% were last listed Active.
  • Removed while Active: a removal whose last published status was Active/Current (the board published no terminal status).
  • Status change / name change: on a license present in both snapshots.
  • Renewal re-listing (relist): an appearance whose issue date is more than 90 days before the earlier snapshot, or is blank / later than the snapshot in a board that publishes issue dates. Excluded from every headline count; kept in the CSV with the triggering date or reason in detail.
  • Relicensing flag: a removal whose normalized street address reappears among the same window's new licenses in the same city (probable ownership change or relocation).
  • Nonresident flag: license held by a pharmacy located outside the board's state.

What the full archive contains

  • Raw snapshots: every board file exactly as served, gzip-stored under <source>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/, with a per-source manifest line per fetch (UTC timestamp, filename, SHA-256, size, stored / unchanged / failed). A day on which a board republished a byte-identical file stores nothing and records "unchanged"; a failed fetch is recorded as such. Every fetch attempt is logged, so the capture history is auditable rather than a derived view.
  • Cadence: Texas daily; the other jurisdictions weekly (self-healing: a weekly source more than 7 days stale runs on the next daily invocation). Two independent recorders (a local machine and a cloud job) capture Texas so a missed day on one is covered by the other.
  • Normalized panel: per jurisdiction, per snapshot, the parsed roster keyed by license number with name, status, type/class, city, state, original issue date and normalized street address; and the derived event log with the flags above.
  • Texas 2014-2026: the 49 distinct Internet Archive captures (digest-deduplicated from 75), the per-license and per-event tables, county normalization, and the chain / independent classification.
  • Coverage: 12 jurisdictions (11 states + DC) from 2026-08-08; Florida, Nevada, Mississippi and Pennsylvania from 2026-08-16 (16 today); Oregon and Nebraska PDF rosters recorded from 2026-08-16 pending parsers. Each addition is dated - a jurisdiction's history starts the day it was first recorded, never earlier, except Texas.

The full archive, ongoing feed, and per-jurisdiction history extracts are available for research collaboration and licensing: open a data request.
